🎯 AI UI GENERATION PROMPT
Architecture & Interior Design Portfolio Website
Role

You are a senior UI/UX designer specializing in premium architecture studio websites.
Your task is to design a mobile-first, highly responsive website UI/UX for an Architecture & Interior Design Consultant.

The focus is purely on design, layout, visual hierarchy, and user experience — NOT on technical implementation or code.

1. Primary Design Goal

Create a premium, timeless, calm, and professional visual experience that showcases architectural and interior design work in an elegant, structured, and visually dominant manner.

The website should feel like a high-end architecture studio portfolio, where:

Visuals are the hero

Text is minimal and supportive

The design communicates trust, design excellence, and credibility
